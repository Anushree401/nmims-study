IVP: Master Problem-Solving & Numericals Guide

A complete, systematic mathematical reference gathering and organizing every single numerical problem archetype covered in the NMIMS Image and Video Processing syllabus and past university examinations. For every archetype, this guide details recognition triggers, formulas, step-by-step calculation protocols, units, common exam traps, and sanity checks.

Core IVP Numerical Archetypes

Complete Formula & Solution Protocols
Archetype 1

Distance Metrics & Shortest Path Traversal

Formulas: \(D_e = \sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2}\), \(D_4 = |\Delta x| + |\Delta y|\), \(D_8 = \max(|\Delta x|, |\Delta y|)\).
m-Path Rule: Never take diagonal jump if shared 4-neighbor belongs to \(V\)!

Archetype 2

Histogram Equalization Formula Protocol

Step-by-Step Algorithm:

  1. Compute PDF: \(p_r(r_k) = \frac{n_k}{M \cdot N}\).
  2. Compute Cumulative CDF: \(\sum_{j=0}^k p_r(r_j)\).
  3. Multiply by maximum level: \((L - 1) \times \text{CDF}\).
  4. Round to nearest integer: \(s_k = \text{round}((L - 1) \cdot \text{CDF})\).

Archetype 3

2D Matrix Transforms (Hadamard & DCT)

Master Transform Formula: $$\mathbf{F = A \cdot f \cdot A^T}$$ For \(2 \times 2\) Hadamard / DCT: \(A = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}\).
Always verify Parseval's energy conservation: \(\sum f_{ij}^2 = \sum F_{uv}^2\).

Archetype 4

Sobel & Laplacian Spatial Convolutions

Mask Placement & Sum of Products: Compute \(G_x\) and \(G_y\). Magnitude \(M = \sqrt{G_x^2 + G_y^2}\). Direction \(\alpha = \tan^{-1}(G_y / G_x)\).

Archetype 5

Otsu's Optimal Between-Class Variance Calculation

Master Formula: $$\sigma_B^2(k) = P_1(k) P_2(k) [m_1(k) - m_2(k)]^2 = \frac{[m_G P_1(k) - m(k)]^2}{P_1(k)[1 - P_1(k)]}$$ Compute global mean \(m_G\) once. Find \(k\) that maximizes \(\sigma_B^2\).

Archetype 6

Video Block Matching (EBMA vs. TSS)

Full Search Evaluations: \((2p + 1)^2\). For \(p = 7 \implies 225\) checks.
Three-Step Search (TSS): \(9 + 8 + 8 = 25\) checks (\(9\times\) speedup!).
